# FMCSA Carrier Leads audit — November 25, 2026

This report covers the November 25 changes through [commit dbd61a9](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/commit/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9).

## Findings

- **High · A paid run can suppress leads that were never delivered.** The Apify SDK can push zero records after the charge limit is reached. The Actor replaces a zero charged count with the full output length, then saves those lead IDs in the tracking store. A subsequent `onlyNew` run skips them. Persist only confirmed delivered IDs and exercise the zero-budget and partial-budget paths. [Batch delivery and tracking](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/src/main.py#L95-L109)
- **High for buyers of new authority grants · The “new authorities” feed measures new USDOT registrations.** It selects active census records by the date added, then labels each as a new authority. Private carriers can hold a USDOT number without operating authority, and existing registrants can acquire new authority later. Sell this as a registration feed or join official authority-grant history on the effective date. [Query and label](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/src/fmcsa.py#L305-L330) · [FMCSA’s distinction](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/faq/what-operating-authority-mc-number-and-who-needs-it)
- **Medium · The default contact filter drops cell-only leads.** The result mapper accepts a cell number, but the upstream contact filter checks only phone and email. Include the cell field in the query. [Contact mapping](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/src/fmcsa.py#L155) · [Upstream predicate](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/src/fmcsa.py#L313-L315)
- **Commercial · The $49/month digest button ends at a README.** The target describes delivery to an inbox or Sheet. It provides no checkout, subscription form, or signup contact. Wire the button to a working purchase or inquiry destination. [Buyer button](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/public/index.html#L253) · [Destination](https://github.com/Shoomaii/fmcsa-carrier-leads-actor/blob/dbd61a9e8755764a4e02c137108515e03df14eb9/README.md#L35-L37)

## Verification

All six FMCSA tests passed, including the live U.S. DOT integration test. The billing finding comes from reading the batch handler and the Apify SDK’s charge-limit behavior. Live paid billing and digest fulfillment remain unverified.
